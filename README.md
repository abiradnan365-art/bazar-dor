# বাজার দর | BazarDor

# BazarDor is a web application that helps users explore the latest prices of daily essential products in Bangladesh. Users can view product prices, comapare prices across different markets, and make better purchasing decision with ease.

# Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS , REST API, Better Auth, MongoDB

1 Daily Product Prices – Explore the prices of essential products in Bangladesh.
2 Market Price Comparison – Compare product prices across different markets and divisions.
3 Product Details – View detailed information about individual products, including their prices and market data.
4 User Authentication – Sign up, sign in, and securely manage your account.
5 Responsive Design – Enjoy a smooth browsing experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
